Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

DocDash helps you prepare practical journals and work with PDF and document files. This policy explains what happens when you use this website.

Your files

Journal sheets, PDFs, images and Word documents you select are processed in your browser. The DocDash app does not upload those files to its own server. Generated files are downloaded to your device. Avoid using the site with documents you are not permitted to process.

Information saved in your browser

DocDash uses your browser storage for convenience: saved subject profiles and sheet-position preferences are stored in local storage on your device. A session storage entry helps the visitor counter avoid counting the same browser session repeatedly. These values are not sent to DocDash by the app. You can remove them by clearing this site's storage in your browser settings.

Usage counter

The visitor and generated-journal counters use the Abacus counting service at abacus.jasoncameron.dev. When enabled, DocDash sends a counter request containing the configured namespace and counter name. The counter provider may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address, as part of handling that request. The counter does not receive your documents. If the service is unavailable, the counters may not display.

Fonts and hosting

The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which receives a request from your browser when the font is needed. The hosting provider serving this website may process standard web-server logs, such as IP address, requested file and request time. Their handling is governed by their own privacy policies.

Cookies and advertising

The DocDash app does not set advertising cookies and does not include ad trackers. Browser storage described above is used for app preferences and the session counter.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the website or its services change. The latest version will be available on this page. For questions, contact the website owner through the channel where you received this website.
